Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Is CAERING waterproof?

Fully. Rated 5 ATM, the ring is comfortable in the shower, the pool and the sea. No charging ports, nothing to seal.

Will I need to charge it every night?

No. One charge lasts about seven days. Most people charge it while getting ready — it's usually full before their coffee.

Does it have a screen, lights or vibrations?

None at all. CAERING believes care is quiet. It never lights up in the night, never buzzes, never judges.

How accurate are the readings?

Twin-channel PPG optics and research-validated algorithms deliver medical-grade signal accuracy, independently tested in sleep laboratories.

Which phones work with CAERING?

Any iPhone on iOS 15 or later, and Android phones on Android 9 or later, connected securely over Bluetooth 5.3.

How do I find my size?

We send a free at-home sizing kit before your ring ships, with a personal fit consultation if you'd like one.

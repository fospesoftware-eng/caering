CAERING One

A ring that knows you, without saying a word.

The brushed-silver capsule set into the band carries an engraved ECG waveform — CAERING's signature, and the contact point for on-demand heart-rhythm readings. Around it, twin optical channels and a precision thermistor work continuously while you live normally.

  • Twin-channel PPG with green, red and infrared emitters
  • ECG electrode with medical-grade signal chain
  • Skin-temperature sensing to ±0.1 °C
  • On-device processing — readings stay private
